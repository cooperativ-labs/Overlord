# ADR 0009: Conformance manifests pin the major version

- Date: 2026-11-12
- Status: accepted
- Contract version: 168.0.0
- Supersedes: none

## Context

Each shipped component declares in its `conformance-manifest.yaml` the
contract version it was validated against. With integer versions, every
contract change made every manifest stale, and keeping 16 manifests current
across two repositories on every patch would be churn without information.

## Decision

A conformance manifest declares, as a quoted strict Semantic Version, the
contract version it was validated against. `yarn check:conformance-versions`
requires the same MAJOR as `contract/VERSION` and a version not newer than it;
an earlier MINOR or PATCH of the same major is accepted and listed. A major
increment therefore requires restamping every manifest. Manifests generated by
`yarn connectors:capabilities` take the current version. The CLI's
stale-install comparison in `cli/src/connectors.ts` compares the major version
and reads a former integer version `N` as `N.0.0`.

On adoption every manifest in this repository was restamped to `168.0.0`.
`OverlordMobile` keeps its integer manifest until the phase 5 baseline.

## Consequences

- Patch and minor contract changes leave hand-maintained manifests alone.
- A manifest from an earlier major fails the check instead of only warning.
- A connector installed against `168` is not reported stale against a
  manifest declaring `168.0.0`.

## Alternatives considered

- Require every manifest to equal `VERSION`: rejected for the churn above.
- Accept any earlier version with a warning, as before: rejected because a
  manifest from an earlier major may rely on a guarantee that no longer holds.
